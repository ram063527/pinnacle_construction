## Rules for anything added here

- Abstract, textural, or process-and-hands only. No identifiable faces.
- Never adjacent to a named project, a project card, the Projects listing, a project
  detail page, or the home hero.
- Nothing that could be read as "this is what your flat will look like."
- Always treated (crimson duotone, hard crop, dark overlay) so it reads editorial
  rather than documentary.
