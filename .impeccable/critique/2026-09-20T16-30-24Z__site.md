# The four user proposals

(a) Hero carousel: reject the straightforward version. Notre Dame data: first slide took around 84% of hero clicks, total hero engagement around 1%. The hero currently carries headline, subhead and both CTAs, all of which would sit on moving ground. LCP cost: either preload four images on 4G or stall transitions. It also solves the wrong problem, which is that Featured Projects is buried. Do instead, in order: (1) keep the static hero, move Featured Projects directly under the stats bar; (2) add a static render strip of 4-5 clickable thumbnails pinned to the hero bottom; (3) only if insisted, slow crossfade 8s or longer, no transform, static text layer, first image priority, rest lazy, reduced-motion suppressed, visible pause.

(b) Pexels imagery: modify. Legitimate for abstract and textural work (concrete, rebar, blueprint, scaffolding) on Services, and people/process on About. Never on or near a project card, listing, detail page or hero, and never anything readable as "this is what your flat will look like." Treat all stock editorially (duotone crimson, hard crop, overlay) so it reads non-literal. Add a credit field; the finish standard requires provenance on every shipping raster.

(c) About page: recommend rebuild. Full-bleed completed-project render replacing the flat crimson band. Our Story as a dated timeline against imagery. Drop or reframe the duplicated stats bar. Cut the duplicated Why Choose Us and put an eight-card completed-projects strip in its slot. Mission/Vision against a textural background with the heading colour split fixed. Delete team now, one real line instead. End on a testimonial plus CTA. Needs no new photography.

(d) Navbar separation: confirmed and diagnosed. app/components/Header.js:30-32. Three causes stack: border-b is border-transparent until scrollY is over 8; shadow-sm is gated on the same flag; and bg-surface/90 is the same colour as the page's bg-surface, so the alpha produces no tonal step and backdrop-blur has nothing to blur. Separation depends entirely on scroll state, and the design assumes every page opens with a dark full-bleed hero. True on Home, About and project detail. False on /projects, /contact, /privacy.

Fix: make border-border unconditional and switch to bg-surface-raised/95 for a real tonal step in both themes; keep shadow-sm on scroll. Accept the permanent hairline over the Home hero rather than adding route-conditional header logic. While in the file: add an active nav state via usePathname(), and replace the logo JPEG (baked white box) with a transparent PNG or SVG, which also removes the dark:brightness-110 hack.
