## Scope

Shared visual world for the whole site. Home is the primary/first surface; About, Projects, Project Detail, Contact, and Privacy Policy inherit this world rather than each running their own direction round. Visitor mode: Persuade (Projects/Project Detail carry a secondary Operate-like search/filter/compare task inside the Persuade shell).

## Audience, job, proof, constraints

Nagpur families evaluating a home purchase. Job: find a matching project, trust the builder, then book a site visit or contact directly. Real proof on hand: 15 years/43 projects/521 clients stats, real testimonials and team bios (no photos), real brochures/renders/RERA numbers. Constraints: no CMS (static data files), Cloudinary media, YouTube-embedded video reels, static Google Maps iframe, light/dark theme (auto-detect + persisted toggle), English only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Earn trust the way a well-run developer's own site does — competent, professional real-estate marketing, not a novel format. Refuses gimmicks in favor of doing the genre extremely well: legible hierarchy, confident photography, clear conversion paths.

OWN-WORLD: Full-palette color strategy with both brand colors as named roles: Pinnacle Blue (primary — nav, headings, primary buttons, links) and Pinnacle Crimson (accent — status badges, highlights, secondary CTAs like WhatsApp/Book-a-Visit), against a warm-neutral light theme and a charcoal/near-black dark theme (never pure white or pure black). Headings in Sora, body/UI text in Inter. Cards carry a consistent corner radius and soft elevation; full-bleed render photography drives heroes and project cards.

STORY: A visitor lands, immediately understands Pinnacle is an established, trustworthy Nagpur builder (stats, why-choose-us), then either browses to a matching project by name/location/configuration or goes straight to contact. A project page gives everything needed to decide: renders, floor plans, distances, RERA status, and one-tap paths to book a visit or start a WhatsApp chat.

FIRST VIEWPORT (home): full-bleed hero render, headline and subhead over it, one primary CTA, with the stats bar (43 projects / 521 clients / 21 team / 15 years) immediately below the fold, not buried further down the page.

FORM: Classic real-estate site — the standing exit/canon path, chosen directly over the assigned direction (chat-native catalog, seed key cef125a2) and its challengers. Held to the craft level of Godrej Properties and Prestige Group.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
